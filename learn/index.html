Machine & Deep Learning

An interactive way to learn ML and DL.

Machine learning and deep learning from the basics to advanced topics, in one place, for someone starting out. The pages are in learning order: the maths first, then classic algorithms, then neural networks up to vision and multimodal transformers and how to make them small and fast. Each step opens with a timeline of how the field got from its first idea to today's state of the art. Each one runs in your browser, so you can try the ideas rather than just read about them.
